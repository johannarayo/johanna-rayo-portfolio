Youth Champions

A facilitator onboarding program, rebuilt end to end

The challenge

Facilitators are external, work in different roles and join on different timelines. Each one needed a clear, repeatable path from signing on to running their first workshop.

What I built

  • An onboarding roadmap restructured into three phases, with a visual journey map and a matching process document
  • A nine-video onboarding library, reviewed and re-scripted from scratch
  • A 68-page Facilitator Success SOP manual covering 34 SOPs across 15 functional areas
  • A master onboarding tracker organized by stage, with formula validation so status stays accurate

Youth Champions

A weekly rhythm for Lead Facilitators

The challenge

Lead Facilitators at two levels guide the rest of the team. Their weekly meeting produced good ideas, but decisions and feedback needed a reliable place to land.

What I built

  • Structured documents turned from meeting transcripts, capturing the Monday Facilitator Forum format, a week-ahead teaching model and feedback capture
  • A clear split between actions I could take straight away and decisions that needed leadership, with ready-to-send emails drafted for each
  • A 24-week curriculum links template, split by Fall and Spring, so Lead Facilitators always have the right materials
  • Facilitator evaluation, mentoring, observation and check-in systems that support coaching between sessions

The weekly cycle

Monday ForumLead Facilitators meet and review the week
Week-ahead teachingNext week’s content walked through in advance
Feedback capturedNotes and observations logged in one place
Actions and decisionsSorted, assigned and followed up

Then it repeats the next Monday, so nothing raised in the forum gets lost.
